### Part A: Two Core Feature Suggestions for Coy

1. **Anonymous Mutual-Interest Nudge ("Warm Ping")**
* **What it is:** A privacy-safe prompt tied to a crush slot that notifies a recipient—or invites an unjoined contact via an invite link—that someone has added them on Coy, without revealing who the sender is or confirming whether interest is mutual. Recipients only discover a match if they also add that person's number and the pairing is mutual.

* **Why it is required:** Coy's core promise is never exposing unreciprocated interest unless a match is mutual, but relying purely on chance that a crush joins and adds the user's phone number creates high cold-start friction. A zero-leak nudge improves **invite conversion** and **retention** by giving recipients a reason to complete phone OTP onboarding and add their own crushes while keeping one-sided interest completely hidden.


2. **Crush Slot Cooldown & Rotation (with Verified Account Signals)**
* **What it is:** A slot-management flow that lets users release or archive an unreciprocated crush slot after a set cooldown window—complementing invite-based and paid extra slots—while reinforcing account continuity through email verification. Slot recycling never reveals who added whom unless mutual, keeping the underlying privacy rules intact.

* **Why it is required:** When crush slots are limited and expanded through referrals or extra-slot unlocks, holding onto stagnant slots can frustrate active users whose crushes never join or aren't interested. Allowing periodic slot rotation alongside clear email verification strengthens **retention**, reduces slot-lock fatigue, and increases **conversion** on extra slots because users spend extra capacity on active possibilities rather than dead slots.
